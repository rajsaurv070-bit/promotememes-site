      How it works

      1. Paste your token address and check it.
      2. Pick a package: a free 15-minute listing, or a paid listing of up to 24 hours.
      3. Pay with your wallet. Your listing goes live as soon as the payment is confirmed on Solana.

      Questions

      How long does a listing run?

      As long as the package you pick, from 15 minutes up to 24 hours. A countdown shows in Live promotions.

      How is my payment checked?

      The page reads the Solana network and checks that the right amount of SOL reached the payment address. No account or email is needed.

      Can I use the free listing again?

      Each token gets one free listing every 24 hours.

      Is a listing financial advice?

      No. A listing is promotion, not an endorsement. Always do your own research before buying any token.
